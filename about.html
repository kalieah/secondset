ABOUT SECONDSET

Economics is
everywhere.

SecondSet is a personal project exploring the economics behind the decisions, trends, and systems that shape everyday life.

01 — SECONDSET

Economics beyond
the textbook.

SecondSet is a space where economics meets everyday life.

From the things we buy to the prices we see, the businesses we interact with, and the decisions we make, economics is constantly happening around us.

Through articles and independent research, secondset tries to break down those ideas and make them easier to understand.

02 — THE PERSON BEHIND IT

I'm Gülşah Doğaç, a high school student exploring economics, business, and the ideas behind everyday decisions.

03 — WHY I STARTED

I wanted to look
closer.

I started secondset because I wanted to understand economics through things I actually see and experience every day.

Instead of starting with complicated theories, I wanted to start with simple questions.

Questions like why we buy things we don't need, why prices change, or why certain trends suddenly become popular.

The goal is to take those everyday moments and look at what is happening underneath them.

04 — WHAT I'M EXPLORING

different ideas.
